HOW TO PLAY

MOVE

WASD or arrow keys move your survivor around the arena. The camera follows you.

FIGHT

Stay on the roads. Aim with your mouse and hold the left button to fire. Release it for automatic targeting. SPACE fires a bonus shot. City blocks stop movement and bullets.

COLLECT

Defeated enemies drop experience shards. Walk over them to pick them up.

LEVEL UP

Filling the experience bar levels you up. The game pauses so you can pick an upgrade.

UPGRADE

Choose one of three random upgrades each level. Builds get stronger as the run goes on.

SURVIVE

Hold out for 10 minutes, then defeat the boss that arrives. That's the whole objective.

CHOOSE

Before each run, pick a survivor. Each one trades a strength for a weakness, so builds play differently from the start.
